Trackbit is a simple and user-friendly tool designed to help you build healthy habits, stay consistent, and track your progress. You can create habits, organize them into categories, set reminders, and keep track of your streaks!

༺♡༻ ─˖˙ ⋆ ✮about trackbit✮ ⋆ ˙˖─ ༺♡༻

I created this to make building good habits feel easier and more motivating. Instead of focusing on perfection, the goal is to encourage small, consistent improvements every day!!

  • Track Habits — Add and manage your daily or weekly habits.
  • Streaks — Keep track of how many days you've stayed consistent.
  • Analytics — See your progress based on different habit categories.
  • Edit Habits — Change your habit's name, category, or frequency.
  • Categories — Organize habits into Health, Productivity, Learning, or your own categories.
  • Reminders — Set reminders so you don't forget your habits.
